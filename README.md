# 💰 Smart AI Expense Manager

An intelligent, personal finance tracking web application built with **Python** and **Streamlit**. This app doesn't just record your expenses; it uses **Machine Learning** to predict your future spending trends and helps you stay within your budget.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📈 Interactive Dashboard
Get a bird's-eye view of your finances with real-time metrics:
* **Total Spend**
* **Average Expense**
* **Transaction Count**

### 📊 Visual Analytics
* **Category-wise Split:** A donut chart to see exactly where your money goes (Food, Shopping, Rent, etc.).
* **Daily Trends:** A bar chart to track your spending habits over time.

### 🎯 Smart Budgeting
Set a monthly budget and get proactive alerts:
* 🟡 **Yellow Warning:** When you hit 80% of your budget.
* 🔴 **Red Alert:** When you exceed your limit.

### 🤖 AI Forecasting
Uses **Scikit-Learn (Linear Regression)** to analyze historical data and predict your next month's estimated expenses.

### 📉 Trend Analysis
Visualizes an AI trend line against your actual spending to show if your expenses are stabilizing or increasing.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python
* **Library:** Streamlit, Pandas, Plotly, Scikit-Learn
* **Model:** Linear Regression
